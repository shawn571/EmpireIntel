## 1b. Confirmed by the client: the 65 challenges (run 8, EI-E-22)

The game's own list agrees with section 1 in every group:
- **Classes:** 27 challenges (Novice 25, Experienced 45, Master 60, "for the first time on a <class>").
- **Crafting:** 18 challenges (Journeyman 150, Expert 225, Artisan 300, "for the first time").
- **PvP ranks:** 5 (Sergeant (3) through Grand Marshal / High Warlord (14)).
- **Battleground exalted:** 4 (Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin, Warsong Gulch and **Darkspear Islands**).
- **Field of Honor:** weeks 4, 7, 10.
- **Spelunkers:** 3 (6, 10 and 17 dungeons).
- **Raids:** 3 (13, 8 and 1 bosses).
- **Adventure:** 2.

Explorer needs the Explore Azeroth achievement on one character: Eastern Kingdoms (23 zones, including Riverglades) and Kalimdor (20 zones, including Mount Hyjal and Shen'dralas). The class and crafting rules that P2-3 labels "from your list" are now **confirmed by the game**.

## 1a. Confirmed by the client and the pane (2026-11-08)

- The pane's total is **65** ("Legacy Points 1 / 65") [screenshot], which matches section 1.
- The groups and sub-groups are the achievement categories in EI-E-20 [client]. Classes holds the 9 classes; Tradeskills holds exactly Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring (confirms Q-P2-3a); Player vs. Player holds Ranks, Reputations and Season Journey; Adventure holds Explorer, Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor; Dungeons; Raids.
- **Explorer:** "Have a character with the Explore Azeroth achievement" [screenshot]. **Lord Valthalak Laid to Rest:** "Complete the questline beginning with An Earnest Proposition, and ending with Saving the Best for Last" [screenshot].
- **Raids** (3 challenges, boss kills) [screenshot, 2026-11-08]:
  - **Conqueror of the Wilds:** defeat 13 encounters in **Hyjal Summit**: Bandalar, Time-Lost Battalion, Old Gloomlurker, Kathris the Haunted, Elder Minderel, Council of Thorns, The Wild King, Ancient of Decay, Sylvestris Dusksong, Gharalis the Abyssal, Anara Chillwind, Tracker Stillwind, Nythus the Dreambound.
  - **Conqueror of the Deeps:** defeat 8 encounters in **the Barrow Deeps**: Chillhowl, Khalith the Dreadspinner, Amethrax, Ravus and Darlissa, Elder Tangleclaw, Well of Sorrow, Del'lynar Songwood, Sonya Darkhallow.
  - **Conqueror of the Lair:** defeat Onyxia.
- The client also lists a category **"Tier 1 Gear"** under Raids that the pane does not show. It is not a raid challenge; the user confirmed the Raids page holds only the three boss-kill challenges. Like "Do Not Display", it is read but kept out of the totals.
- Per-challenge details (criteria such as each dungeon or boss) come with the 0.1.7 read; the read's criteria will be checked against these lists.

## 2. What the screenshots show [screenshot]

- **Progress Track:** "Legacy Points 1" (C1 Paladin 25: the class-25 challenge, as the estimate predicted). Rewards at **15** (Holstered Replica Ironforge Air Rifle), **25** (Spectral Bear Cub), **40** (Spectral Bear Tabard) and **55** (Reins of the Spectral Bear).
- **Legacy Tree:** "Available points: 1"; three trees, each with its own spent-points counter (0) and **25 ranks in 9 nodes**:
  - **Professions:** 5, 5, 5, 2, 1, 3, 1, 1, 2 (the two 0/1 "?" nodes are hidden)
  - **Adventure:** 2, 5, 5, 1, 1, 5, 3, 1, 2 (two hidden)
  - **Resourcefulness:** 5, 3, 2, 1, 1, 2, 1, 5, 5 (two hidden)
  - In all, 27 nodes and 75 ranks. Arrows show prerequisites; the green-bordered nodes are the available ones (2 or 3 per tree). "Apply Changes" commits a spend.
- [web] A spending cap of 16 points per character at launch; perk names such as Overtime, Productivity Bonus, Diligent Study, Bountiful Harvest (Professions), Thrill of Adventure, Well Rested (Adventure), Reinforcement, Diplomat (Resourcefulness). Names and values differ between sites.
